FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for clients who need to understand the rental process, collection details, and how to choose the right setup.

Q1

How do I book a rental?

Browse the gear, submit the booking enquiry form, or contact us on WhatsApp. We confirm the dates, advise the best setup if needed, and arrange collection.

Q2

Can you recommend gear for my shoot?

Yes. Tell us what you are filming, where the shoot is happening, and what type of result you want. We can suggest the right camera, drone, or accessory combination.

Q3

Do you provide beginner guidance?

Yes. We can explain basic setup, key functions, and handling tips so first-time renters feel more confident before the shoot starts.

Q4

Which areas do you cover?

We coordinate rentals through our Penang HQ and service points in Kuala Lumpur, Johor Bahru, and Kota Kinabalu, with additional coverage available depending on request.

Q5

What if I am not sure how long I need the gear?

Send us your tentative schedule first. We can help you estimate the right rental duration based on setup time, shooting days, and return timing.

Q6

Where can I contact your team?

Use the contact section at the bottom of the main page or message us on WhatsApp for the fastest response.
