Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 94 Ezy Rent may collect, use, store, and protect information when you browse our website, submit an enquiry, contact us through WhatsApp or email, or proceed with a rental booking.

Information We May Collect

We may collect contact details and booking-related information that you provide directly, such as your name, phone number, email address, rental dates, delivery or pickup location, selected equipment, and enquiry details. We may also receive technical information such as browser type, device information, and basic website usage data.

How We Use Information

  • Respond to rental enquiries and confirm availability.
  • Prepare quotations, bookings, delivery or pickup arrangements, and customer support.
  • Improve the website, rental experience, and customer communication.
  • Maintain business records, prevent misuse, and comply with applicable legal or payment requirements.

Sharing And Disclosure

We do not sell customer information. Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to operate the business, such as with payment providers, delivery partners, technical service providers, or when required by law, regulation, fraud prevention, or dispute handling.

Data Retention And Security

We keep information only for as long as needed for enquiries, bookings, customer service, record-keeping, or compliance purposes. We take reasonable administrative and technical steps to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Cookies And Analytics

The website may use basic analytics, cached content, or standard browser technologies to improve performance and understand website usage. You may adjust your browser settings to manage cookies where available.

Contact

If you would like to request an update, correction, or removal of personal information related to a booking or enquiry, please contact us at 94renteasy@gmail.com or through WhatsApp.
