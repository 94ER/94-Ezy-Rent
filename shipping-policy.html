Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 2026

94 Ezy Rent (94好租) supports self-pickup and selected delivery arrangements for camera, drone, and creator-gear rentals. Delivery availability, timing, and charges may vary depending on the order, rental dates, location, and equipment requested.

Coverage And Delivery Method

Orders may be fulfilled through self-pickup, local courier, Lalamove, or other agreed delivery methods depending on the requested location and operational availability. Cross-border or special-area requests are handled case by case.

Processing And Scheduling

  • Delivery or pickup timing is confirmed only after the booking is accepted by our team.
  • Customers should provide accurate contact, location, and timing details before dispatch.
  • Urgent or same-day requests are subject to stock, route, and driver availability.

Delivery Fees

Applicable delivery, return-trip, or handling fees are shared during the quotation or booking confirmation stage. Fees may vary based on area, timing, urgency, parking requirements, or special handling needs.

Customer Responsibilities

  • Be reachable at the confirmed contact number during the arranged delivery or return window.
  • Ensure the delivery or pickup location is safe and accessible.
  • Inspect the equipment promptly upon receipt and notify us as soon as possible if there is any issue.

Failed Delivery Or Changes

If the recipient is unavailable, if location details are inaccurate, or if timing changes after dispatch, additional rescheduling or re-delivery charges may apply. We reserve the right to re-arrange the timing where operationally necessary.

Returns

Returned equipment should be handed back using the agreed method and schedule. Late returns, inaccessible pickup points, or missed handovers may affect the final charges or next-rental scheduling.

Specific delivery lead times, courier method, and charges should still be confirmed in your actual booking conversation or invoice before payment is completed.
